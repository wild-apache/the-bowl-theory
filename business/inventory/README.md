# Inventory

Planned scope:

- Ingredient and packaging catalogue with consistent units.
- Opening stock, receipts, consumption, adjustments, and closing stock.
- Reorder thresholds and low-stock views.
- Batch/expiry tracking if required by the operating process.
- Audit trail for adjustments.

Keep supplier contact details, purchase invoices, and commercially sensitive stock reports in controlled systems rather than public source files. Define unit conversions before implementing automated stock calculations.
