# Analytics

Planned metrics:

- Orders and units sold by product, pack size, date, and channel.
- Gross sales, discounts, refunds, and net sales.
- Ingredient and packaging costs.
- Platform/payment fees and estimated contribution margin.
- Wastage, stock-outs, and reorder performance.

Every metric needs a written definition and source of truth. Reports containing customer-level data or non-public financial results must require authentication and must not be committed to this public repository.
