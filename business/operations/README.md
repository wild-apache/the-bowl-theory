# Kitchen operations

Planned operating workflows:

- Daily opening and closing checklist.
- Prep planning and batch-yield recording.
- Food preparation, packing, and dispatch checklist.
- Order status definitions and hand-off responsibilities.
- Cleaning and maintenance logs where useful.

Operational checklists should be practical, timestamped where needed, and easy for staff to follow on a phone. Food safety and local compliance requirements must be confirmed for the actual operating location before launch.
